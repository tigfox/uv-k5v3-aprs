The screen

162.550 the frequency, big, at the top. The tone mark and tone value sit just under it.

KD2ABC the channel name (the callsign), big, under the frequency.

Mechanicsburg the city, then the landmark: the channel's place text, big, scrolling if it is long.

The place text hides while a signal is received (the signal bar takes its row); the name stays. A channel with no place text shows only the name. The name shows only if the channel display mode (menu) is Frequency; the other modes print it themselves.

Banks (scan lists)

A bank is a scan list. ▲ ▼ step through the channels of the active bank only. ALL shows every channel.

Banks in the default file: WX weather, FRS, GMR GMRS, MUR MURS, REP repeaters. The radio cycles through banks that have channels, then MIX and ALL.

Two key actions

Assign them in the menu's key settings (F1 / F2 / M, short or long press): BANK and TONE SEARCH.

BANK

  1. Press the key. The radio moves to the next bank and shows its name (for example BANK REP) for about 1.5 seconds.
  2. It lands on the channel you last left that bank on (kept until the radio is switched off).
  3. While scanning, it only changes the bank being scanned.

TONE SEARCH

  1. Select the repeater's channel and wait for it to be transmitting.
  2. Press the key: SEARCHING PL. It listens for up to about 16 seconds.
  3. Found: TX PL 100.0 (or TX DCS 023). That becomes the channel's transmit tone and is saved. The receive tone is not touched.
  4. Nothing: NO TONE FOUND. Any key, or PTT, stops the search.

It needs a steady carrier for a second or two. A short kerchunk will not be identified.

Loading channels (computer)

  1. Make a CSV (RepeaterBook export in CHIRP format works). Name is the callsign, Comment is the place text (City, Landmark, up to 45 characters; longer is cut). Add a column Scanlist (or Bank): empty = no bank, ALL, a number 1-24, or a bank name (3 characters shown; a new name takes the first unnamed list).
  2. Close CHIRP and any web programmer (only one program can hold the serial port). Radio on, normal mode, cable in.
  3. python3 tools/fmvoice/chirp_cli.py upload-csv FILE.csv --port /dev/cu.usbserial-XXXX
  4. It downloads the radio and keeps it as fmvoice-backup-<time>.img, puts the CSV on top (channels by Location; ones the CSV does not mention stay), asks for yes, uploads. Restart the radio.

In the CHIRP app instead: Help → Developer Mode → File → Load Module (the FM Voice driver), choose Quansheng UV-K1 & UV-K5 V3 (F4HWN FM Voice). The stock driver would drop the place text. Restore a backup with --upload-mmap (see tools/fmvoice/README.md).

Saving a channel on the radio

Use the stock menu (MEM-CH to save the current frequency, MEM-NAME for the name, offset and tone items for the rest). The place text can only be set from the computer. TONE SEARCH sets the transmit tone.

Good to know

  • Place text is kept for channels 1-256 only.
  • A change made to a channel by anything but the FM Voice driver (the stock driver, another firmware) hides its place text until it is uploaded again from the CSV.
  • A settings reset erases the channels and their place text. Keep the backup image.
  • No dual watch and no second VFO in this build.
  • This image keeps its own configuration in its multiboot slot; the APRS and Fusion slots are separate.
